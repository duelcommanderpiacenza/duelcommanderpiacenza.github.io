Pulsante indietro nell'hero — 3 proposte

Base: proposta 2 (barra unica nel colore dell'hero, l'hero appeso sotto). Desktop. L'etichetta è dinamica: la pagina da cui si arriva (qui "Leghe & Eventi", oppure "Giocatori" arrivando direttamente).

1 · Nella barra

The back pill opens the bar, where the logo is on the other pages (the logo comes back on pages without a hero) — a thin divider, then the tabs. The same place as on phones now (header row, logo's spot), so desktop and phone agree. The active tab dims to a glass pill, so the white back pill is the bar's one strong element. The star moves beside the name, as on phones.

A light line at the top of the hero, lined up with the name: the page you came from as a small glass link, then where you are. Reads as the title's own context ("you're in Leghe & Eventi › Mario Rossi") rather than as a button; the bar stays exactly as on every other page. The star beside the name.

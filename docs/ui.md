# UI: nav, animations, commander card, winrate rules

Part of the project notes split out of `CLAUDE.md` (read on demand, not loaded every session). Keep it in step with the code: update it in the same change.

## Nav & responsive breakpoints

- **Computers** — the top bar (see "One top bar" in docs/modules.md): the tabs in one row, scrolling sideways when they don't fit.
- **Any touch device** (`html.nav-mobile`, set by the head script — see CLAUDE.md; desktop-without-touch is the only thing that gets the computer nav) — the nav is the **floating tab bar** (`js/tab-bar.js`, css/6 "Tab bar"; `js/nav-dropdown.js` only runs the scroll-away of `.site-nav`): a solid capsule at the bottom (white, dark mode `#1e1517` like the segmented controls) (`.tab-bar`, built by the script into `.site-nav`, `.has-tab-bar`; no page markup changed), **five sections — Bacheca · Leghe (a trophy; events.html) · Meta · Giocatori · Social** — **each its icon over its name**: the current one's **icon in a red pill** (`.tab-bar-icon`, 56×30px, its `::before`; the hero's colour on hero pages via `.site-nav.has-hero-accent`, in dark mode the pill its deep shade and the name its light one) **filled white** (outline → solid like Google's apps: `fill-opacity` 0 → 1, the house's door and the play triangle cut out in the pill's colour, `--tab-pill`) and **its name red**; **changing section animates**: the new pill opens out from the middle (scaleX 0.3 → 1) as its icon does its own little animation (the house hops, the trophy spins round, the pie turns into place, the people wiggle, the play button pulses — `tab-icon-*`), the old pill closes back in — only once the bar is up (`.tab-bar.is-ready`, two frames after its first sync, so a page's first paint shows it in place), none with reduced motion; the capsule 66px tall (4px padding, 56px tabs — "Torna all'inizio" and the install card 16 + 66 + 12px up); set when `body[data-active-nav]` changes (the router sets it; a MutationObserver), a haptic tick on a tap, tapping the current section's own page scrolls to the top; no section lit on account / privacy. Touch screens have **no footer** (its controls live in account.html's Impostazioni). This is the *only* place safe-area-inset / overscroll-behavior fixes were added. It also covers touch laptops / tablets / iPad on purpose: gating on `max-width: 640px` plus `pointer: coarse` / `hover: none` was tried and proved unreliable on real phones (Samsung Internet) — see gotcha #9 in docs/gotchas.md.
- **"Meta" = Comandanti, Matchups and Archetipi** on every screen: one tab (`.nav-link[data-nav="meta"]` to commanders.html); those three pages and their detail pages (commander.html, archetype.html) have `body[data-active-nav="meta"]`. No visible title (each `<h1>` for screen readers only). The three switch between each other with a segmented control (`nav.profile-tabs.meta-tabs` inside `div.meta-row`, inside `div.meta-head` — links, the current one `.is-active`; a pill bar on a computer, the sticky segmented control on touch screens, where `.meta-row` is what's sticky — the control alone couldn't stick past its wrapper), laid out by CSS alone (identical from the first frame on all three — a script moving things at mount made it jump during the page change). **The filters' button**: on a computer the round `.filter-toggle-btn` at the row's right end (`.meta-head` an `auto minmax(0, 1fr)` grid, the tabs left); on touch screens **the sticky switcher's fourth, narrow segment** (mockup option A — chosen over a line under the switcher listing the active filters, and a round button beside it): `html.nav-mobile .meta-row > .filter-toggle-btn`, a 54px segment at the switcher's right end — `.meta-row` the one card (flex row, radius 18px, the shadow), the switcher its left part (no right edge, `border-radius: 18px 0 0 18px`), a hairline before the segment; the funnel alone, dark, **red with a filter set**, the segment tinted while open; it opens the panel under the card with Giocatori's collapse (css/5 `.filter-panel`). **Moving between the three** (in-app navigation) the row stays still: view-transition names only during the transition — a computer names the whole `.meta-head` (`meta-heading`), touch screens `.meta-row` (`meta-tabs`) — swapped at once (0s) while the rest fades; to or from another page (`:only-child`) they fade like the page (css/6, after the page-fade keyframes); during the Profilo slide they get none (they'd sit above the sliding page). On Matchups the matrix heading's line (`.matchups-toolbar`) isn't shown: "Crea tabella personalizzata" / "Mostra tabella predefinita" sits under the matrix, centred (`#matchups-matrix + #matchups-custom-btn`; its label names the other mode), and the "?" is on the matrix panel's top-left corner (`CORNER_HELP` in renderMatrix; every `[data-matchups-help]` handled by one click listener on `<main>`, the matrix being redrawn). Comandanti's count is in the search placeholder: "Cerca tra N comandanti…".
- **The footer** (computers only): the credits centred, the IT / EN pill and the theme button on the right (`.footer-actions`), the vibration switch in its left corner where there is one (never, in practice — see `haptics.js` in docs/modules.md). A touch layout with the credits left and the buttons together right was tried and dropped. A "tab with a submenu" pattern for the nav (nesting Matchups under Comandanti) was tried and reverted — in git history if a tab ever needs to nest.

## Commander detail page: Scryfall card art

`commander.html` / `js/commander-detail.js` shows the commander's own Scryfall card in the hero's left column (`.profile-card-col.detail-hero-card`; layout in the commander.html row above).

- **Fetch**: `fetchScryfallCard(name)` does a real JSON `fetch()` to `/cards/named?exact=...` (not the `format=image` redirect trick) because a double-faced commander needs both faces' own `image_uris` to flip between. `scryfallCardImages(card)` tells true double-faced cards (`card_faces[].image_uris` on every face) from split / adventure / flip cards (one combined image, `card.image_uris` only) — that's the signal for the flip button, not the mere presence of `card_faces`. Also used for the partner's card. Loaded independently of the page's Supabase data (not awaited): a slow / unreachable Scryfall never blocks the page.
- **Card shape**: `.commander-card-face` has `aspect-ratio: 488 / 680` + `object-fit: cover`, so the box is always Scryfall's card shape (same as the placeholder and the album's slots) whatever image arrives.
- **Placeholder and reveal**: `#commander-card-skeleton` (`.commander-card-skeleton`; white on the hero's art) sits from first paint in the card's spot and size; the card is revealed on its front image's `load` (`revealCard`); no image (Scryfall miss / image error) → `dropCard`, which removes the placeholder and adds `.is-no-card` to the hero, the text then taking the full width.
- **Flip (double-faced cards only)**: a real CSS 3D flip (`.commander-card-flipper` toggling `.is-flipped { transform: rotateY(180deg) }`, both faces stacked as separate `<img>`s with `backface-visibility: hidden`), not a swap-on-click. `perspective` and the entrance animation both live on a dedicated `.commander-card-perspective` wrapper *one level below* `.commander-card-figure`, never on the figure itself — either one on the figure creates a stacking context that broke click-through to the sibling flip button (see gotcha #10 in docs/gotchas.md).
- **Title fit** (shared `js/page-title-fit.js` — every detail hero's `.profile-hero-name`: player, commander, league, event, archetype, badges): `fitTitleToOneLine()` shrinks the name's font-size (down to a 17px floor) when it doesn't fit its line, measured against the text's own natural unwrapped width — not a fixed smaller font-size, so short names stay full-size and only long ones shrink, only as much as they need to; commander-detail.js refits after a pairing change.

## Animation conventions

- **`entrance-fade-up`** (`opacity 0→1` + `translateY(10px)→none`, 380ms `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`) is the default "this just appeared" animation — `.card`, `.stat-tile`, `.pie-chart-wrap` (covers every chart shape, including the SVG line chart, since they all share that wrapper), `.bar-chart-row`, `.badge-card`, `input[type="search"]`, `.admin-topbar h1`, `.ev-tile`, `.matchups-matrix-wrap`. Reach for this one before inventing a new keyframe for "a card/row/tile just rendered".
- **Page-load reveal, all at once**: every public page's `body` starts with class `is-loading`; `body.is-loading * { animation-play-state: paused }` freezes every animated element (anything using `entrance-fade-up` or similar) at its first frame the instant it's inserted, however long before the page is actually "ready". `js/page-loading.js`'s `hidePageLoading()` removes `is-loading` at the same moment it fades out the splash overlay, so every element's entrance plays together, in sync, rather than each finishing invisibly off in the background the moment it happened to render. **Catch: an `animation` shorthand resets `animation-play-state` to running, so an entrance animation declared with a selector that outranks `body.is-loading *` (0,1,1) — an id, two or more classes, or a `:not(#id)` — is *not* paused: it plays and finishes unseen while the page loads. Each of those needs its own `body.is-loading <selector> { animation-play-state: paused }` in the list right after that rule** (css/1, after `body.is-loading *`: today the list pages' search box). **Any new element given an entrance animation must also be added to the `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block right after the stagger `:nth-child` group in css/1 (the one listing `.card`, `.history-panel`, … `.player-card`, `.league-recap-card` — its `animation: none` is `!important` on purpose: most of those elements declare their animation later in the file, which would otherwise win on source order and keep animating for reduced-motion visitors) that sets `animation: none`** on this same list — otherwise a motion-reduced visitor never sees it at all (the animation is disabled, which for a *paused* animation means it stays stuck at its invisible first frame forever, not "instantly visible" as reduced-motion is supposed to mean).
- **Staggered cascade**: a fixed `:nth-child(2)`…`:nth-child(5)`, `:nth-child(n + 6)` selector group (45ms/90ms/135ms/180ms/225ms `animation-delay`, capped at 6 steps rather than queuing a long list one-by-one) is shared by every repeating grid/list of cards — `.stat-tile`, `.badge-card`. Add a new repeating item type to this *same* selector group rather than writing its own stagger rule. (A different stagger convention — inline `style="animation-delay: ${i * 60}ms"` set from JS — is used instead for `.pie-legend-item` and `.bar-chart-row` rows (`js/metagame-chart.js`), since those come from a dynamically-sized array with no natural CSS `:nth-child` cap; match whichever convention fits the situation, don't mix both on one element type.)
- **In-app page changes** (js/router.js, css/6 "Page transitions"): a 220ms cross-fade of the whole screen, the header's controls and the tab bar still on top. **Exception, touch screens only (`html.nav-mobile`; user, like Google's apps): the Profilo page** (account.html, from the header's picture button) slides in from the right as a solid page over the one before (which drifts 25% left and dims), and on back (popstate going back) **or a tap on a tab of the nav** (`fromNav`: the click inside `.site-nav`) slides away to the right uncovering the next page; the picture button on Profilo itself does nothing (router's click handler) — `html.is-slide-in` / `.is-slide-out`, set by `go()` from the target / leaving page (`page.name === "account"`) and `back` (popstate: the entry's `idx` below the current one); the header stays still as on every transition; page parts with their own name (the Meta row) drop it meanwhile, or they'd sit above the sliding page. Other pages, and every page on a computer, keep the cross-fade.
- **Popup/dropdown open**: `cs-menu-in` (custom-select) and `cd-popup-in` (custom-date) — both `opacity 0→1` + `translateY(-4px)→none`, 140ms `ease` — are the shared "small panel opening beneath a trigger" animation (`.matchups-dropdown` reuses `cs-menu-in` directly rather than defining its own). `search-circle-in`/`-out` (mobile full-screen search, see below) is a closer cousin of this same idea for their own, more specific shapes.
- **Commander pairings (commander.html + Comandanti)**: **X + Y is the same pair as Y + X**, whichever seat each was entered in. `commander.html?id=X` is the generic page — every entry with X as main **or** partner ("Tutti"); `&partner=<id>` only the games X played with that partner, `&partner=none` only alone. js/commander-detail.js loads X's entries once, derives the options (an entry's partner = its other commander, `partnerOf`), and `select(key)` redraws everything for one — stat tiles, Utilizzo, Giocatori, Storico partite (`renderSections(entries)`) — with "+ Y" in the title (`.commander-title-partner`, refitted) and Y's card (Scryfall, cached per partner) behind X's (`.detail-hero-card.has-partner`, `.commander-partner-figure`); the address follows with `history.replaceState` (no new history entry). The hero's picker (`#commander-pairings`) shows only for a commander played with a partner at least once, the same on every screen: one glass badge `.pairing-compact` — ‹ "Tutti i partner" / "Da solo" / "con <first part of the partner's name>" › (no dots, no counts); the ‹ › arrows step to the previous / next pairing, round (no swipe / drag gestures — tried and dropped). **Placeholders stay the plain single-card hero** (the page can't know before its data whether a commander has partners); instead, **on one column (≤ 820px) a hero with the picker is exactly as tall as one without** — the picker slimmer (26px arrows, no margins), the hero's rows 12px apart (not 22px) and the card 19px narrower (221px phones / 261px up to 820px), together the picker's room (measured equal); a computer's two-column hero is the card's height either way. **one figure, two places** (`#commander-partner-figure`): with no partner picked it holds the first partner's card tucked behind the commander's, dimmed (the hint there are partners to pick — the commander's card a touch smaller and to the left meanwhile, `.has-pairings:not(.has-partner)`, so it shows inside the column); with one picked (`.has-partner`) it's brought forward beside it — 70% of the column, `top: 1%`, `right: -6%` (clear of the title on a computer) — the commander's card `translateX(-13%) scale(0.88) rotate(-3deg)`, so the partner's name, along a card's top, shows above the commander's. Picking the first partner just moves that same card forward (CSS transitions on top / right / width / transform / filter); another partner: the shown card slides back in behind the commander's (`.is-leaving`, `partner-card-out`, 180ms) and the new one comes out from behind it (`partner-card-in`, 260ms; the moves between the two places 240ms) — showPartnerCard's `wantedId` / `visibleId`. On a computer the name's row (`#commander-hero .profile-hero-heading`) is always a full-size name tall (`calc(clamp(2rem, 4.5vw, 3rem) * 1.05 + 12px)`, the fitted name centred in it), so the name's centre line, the picker and the numbers don't move with the name's length. Picking a pairing, the title (only when its visible text changes) and the picker's label slide in from the arrow's side — › from the right, ‹ from the left — fading in (`.is-swapped-next` / `-prev`, 240ms; not on first render). **The hero keeps its size whatever the pairing**: the commander's card shrinks by a transform (above), never its width, and up to 820px (the hero stacked) the title shows the commander's name alone — "+ partner" wrapped it to a second line there — the badge naming the partner. Comandanti's list groups rows by an order-free pair key (sorted ids); a pair row is led by the one entered as the main commander more often (ties alphabetical) and links `?id=<lead>&partner=<other>`; a solo row links `&partner=none` when that commander also has partnered rows. The charts above it still group by the main commander alone.
- **Hero pages before their colour arrives: neutral, not red** (css/4, after `body.has-hero-accent`): while `main[data-accent-from-hero]` has no `.has-hero-accent` yet (not account.html's signed-out view), `body` redefines the site's red variables to a warm grey (`#5b5552` / `#45403e`; dark mode light-grey text), the desktop bar's gradient (`--bar-c1/-c2`) goes charcoal, and the hero's own starting background and its loading placeholder (`.sk-hero`) are grey too — so taking the art's colour (applyHeroTone, its own 450ms blends) reads as a soft blend from grey, not a jump from red. A page that never gets a colour (no art) stays grey. The red buttons' glows follow their own colour too (`.btn-primary`, account's ✎ `.pc-edit-btn`: `color-mix(in srgb, var(--brand-red) N%, transparent)` — the same red on other pages), not a fixed red. The loading shimmers too: the page placeholders (`.sk`), the commander card's (`.commander-card-skeleton`) and the Album's loading cards (`.cmd-carousel-slide.is-loading`) mix `var(--brand-red)` (dark mode: `var(--brand-red-deep)`, its light shade) — red elsewhere, grey then the hero's colour on hero pages; the hero's own card placeholder stays white on its art, dark mode included. **A new red element's shadow / tint should mix `var(--brand-red)`, never a literal red**, or it stays red on hero pages.
- **Comandanti / Giocatori lists' numbers**: plain labelled numbers (`historyStatsHtml`). Tried and dropped: the record as a green / grey / red bar, the winrate as a pill tinted by value, a small ring before it, a colour-identity strip on the Comandanti rows.
- **Loading placeholders (skeletons) instead of a "Caricamento..." splash**: every public page's `#page-loading-overlay` holds a static `.sk-page` (`aria-hidden`; a modifier per page: `is-home`, `is-events`, `is-list`, `is-matchups`, `is-tiles`, `is-hero` — account.html's with `is-account`: its real hero is in the signed-in view, `display: none` while loading, so the placeholder hero pulls itself up to the bar with its own -28px / -20px) drawing that page's layout as soft shimmering blocks (`.sk` + `.sk-hero` / `.sk-tabs` / `.sk-title` / `.sk-search` / `.sk-row` / `.sk-chart` / `.sk-cols` + `.sk-col` / `.sk-tl` / `.sk-ev-tabs` + `.sk-ev-board` / `.sk-grid` / `.sk-card` / `.sk-matrix`; css/1 right after the overlay rules). Hero pages: `.sk-hero` placed like the real hero (hanging from the bar on a computer; edge to edge under the transparent header on touch screens, `--hero-bleed` wider than 640px), and the touch back pill stays above it (`z-index: 6` while loading). **`.sk-hero.has-card`**: the card's column too, laid out like `.profile-hero-inner` (280px card beside the text; from 820px down the name, the card — 240px on phones, account's 260px — and the numbers, centred) — static on commander.html and account.html (always a card); on player.html set from the first frame by js/card-hints.js (see `player-card.js`), so a hero with a card isn't revealed at the no-card height. The hero's bars follow the real text: the name (`.sk-hero-title`), a `.sk-hero-line` only where the hero has a second line (league's status and dates, event's details; above the name on account, its kicker), the stat chips. `.sk-touch-only` (the segmented controls) is hidden off touch screens; `.sk-cols`' second column only on a computer wider than 860px. The `<p>Caricamento...</p>` stays for screen readers (visually hidden); `.is-stuck` hides the placeholders and shows that message plus the reload button. The shimmer is `background-attachment: fixed` (one wave for the whole screen) and declared as `.page-loading-overlay .sk` so it outranks the `body.is-loading *` pause; none with reduced motion. **A page whose layout changes should have its `.sk-page` markup updated to match.**
- **Data errors (`js/ui.js` `showError`)**: "Si è verificato un errore nel caricamento dei dati." with a **"Riprova"** button under it (`.page-error-box`, `.page-error-retry` → `location.reload()`). Seen mostly when the installed app is reopened or a browser restores the tab while the phone's connection is still coming back: nothing retries a failed Supabase request.
- **Closing/hiding needs its own explicit choreography — CSS cannot transition `display`.** A `display: none ↔ flex/block` toggle (the base state for anything that's conditionally shown) jumps instantly; there's no way to animate *into* that closed state directly. The established pattern is: add an `is-closing` class (distinct from `is-open`) that keeps the element rendered with `display` still set, playing a reverse/exit animation; listen for that animation's `animationend` (or, for the splash overlay, `transitionend`) and only *then* let the element actually disappear (remove the class / let the "closed" `display: none` rule apply again / remove the element outright).
  - `js/page-loading.js`'s `hidePageLoading()` — adds `.is-hidden` (opacity transition), removes the splash element on `transitionend`.
  - `js/info-dialog.js` — the shared pop-up, `.is-closing` + `animationend`; `js/pwa-install.js` the same with `.is-leaving`.
  - `js/site-search.js` — mobile full-screen search overlay, `.is-closing` + `search-circle-out`.
  - Any new dismissible mobile panel/overlay should follow this same `is-open`/`is-closing` + `animationend` shape rather than inventing another way to animate an exit. The animation-based ones check `prefers-reduced-motion` in JS before starting the choreography (not just disabling the animation in CSS) — skipping straight to the closed state — and `page-loading.js` removes the overlay at once when it has no transition duration, because `animationend` never fires for an animation that a reduced-motion CSS rule disabled, which would otherwise leave the element stuck in its `is-closing` (still-rendered) state forever.
- **The ✕ morph** (`search-close-in` / `-x-in` / `-glass-out`): the overlay's ✕ starts as the search button — its spot (`--search-close-dx`), its computed colours (`--search-trigger-bg/-fg`) and its magnifier (a copy injected into `#site-search-close`, `.site-search-close-glass`) — and slides to the header's right end (the account button's spot, `--search-close-right`) turning into the red ✕, the field taking the whole row; the same backwards on close. Because these child animations bubble `animationend`, `close()` only ends `.is-closing` on the overlay's own (`e.target === box`).
- **`search-circle-in`/`-out`** (`js/site-search.js`, mobile only): the full-screen search overlay expands from/collapses back into the round trigger button's own on-screen position via animated `clip-path: circle(... at var(--search-origin-x) var(--search-origin-y))`, rather than a plain fade — reads as growing out of the button that opened it. The origin coordinates and `--search-max-radius` (the exact distance from that point to the farthest viewport corner, guaranteeing full coverage regardless of where the trigger sits) are computed in JS via `getBoundingClientRect()` and set as CSS custom properties on open **and** on close (not cached from open — an orientation change while the overlay is open would otherwise leave it animating from a stale point). The close button is `position: fixed` (`--search-close-top`/`-right`/`-size`) and morphs from the search button (above); `.site-search-field` cascades in a beat later, via `search-content-in` (scale 0.85→1 + fade, same easing curve as the circle itself) rather than the generic `entrance-fade-up` — a slide-up-from-below reads as an unrelated, second animation bolted onto a circular reveal; a scale-in (things coalescing into place) continues the same "expanding from a point" motion instead. Prefer matching an element's own animation's *shape*/vocabulary (expand vs. slide vs. fade) to whatever's already happening around it, not just reusing the nearest existing keyframe by default. (The animation deliberately targets `.site-search-field`, not the whole `.site-search-bar` the close button also lives in markup-wise — see gotcha #12 in docs/gotchas.md: an animated `transform` on an ancestor of a `position: fixed` element hijacks its containing block.)
- **Titles should generally *not* be animated, and should not be hidden/gated behind the page's loading state either.** `.profile-hero-name` (the `<h1>` on every detail hero) deliberately has **no** entrance animation, unlike `.admin-topbar h1` (which keeps `entrance-fade-up`, since admin tab headers are always static text, never swapped). The difference: a detail page's `<h1>` starts as a plain "Caricamento..." placeholder and gets overwritten in place once the entity's name loads — animating that swap, or hiding it until "ready" (both were tried and reverted), makes the transition itself feel like a deliberate, designed part of the page instead of what it actually is: a title that's simply there from the first frame and updates like any other text would. The name genuinely isn't known before that first fetch resolves, so a brief "Caricamento..." is an accepted, deliberate trade-off — not a bug to keep re-solving with cleverer CSS. Default to this same "no animation, no hiding, just let it update" treatment for any other element that's read as *always there* rather than *newly appeared* (page titles, persistent labels) — reserve entrance animations for content that's genuinely new on screen (cards, rows, charts, popups).

## Winrate trend

- Comandanti, Giocatori and Archetipi lists: after the winrate (and the metashare, the same rule) a trend arrow in a small disc tinted in its colour — **↗ (green) / ↘ (red) / → (grey)** — two rules: **Winrate** — now vs. without the commander's / player's **own** latest event within the filters' scope (`latestPresence` in js/history-list.js; events outside the filters never count); → when that was their only event in scope. **Metashare** (its decks ÷ every deck in scope) — **global**: now vs. without the latest event in scope, whoever played in it — ↘ when it had a smaller slice of that event than its average or wasn't in it, ↗ when bigger (from 0% when it was its first); → with a single event in scope. Both computed by subtracting that one event's own numbers (results add up event by event; js/commanders-page.js, js/players-page.js), compared at the shown precision (one decimal, `winrateTrendHtml`); nothing for a "—" winrate. (A per-commander Metashare was tried and dropped: its own last event always holds it, so it nearly always read ↗.) Hangs outside the number's box (`.wr-trend`, absolute) so the number stays centred in its column.

## Winrate sample threshold (Comandanti)

- js/commanders-page.js `MIN_PLAYED_RATIO` = 0.3: a commander's winrate counts only with at least 30% of the matches of the most played commander in the same scope (filters included — relative, so it scales from one event to the whole history). Below it: left out of the Winrate chart, sorted after the others when sorting by winrate, its winrate greyed in the list (`.wr-few`, "Pochi dati" on hover). The chart groups by primary commander, the list by pairing: each computes its own threshold.

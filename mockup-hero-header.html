Hero e intestazione — 3 proposte (desktop)

Ogni proposta: una pagina con hero (giocatore, colore dell'arte: verde acqua) e una senza (Comandanti). Il telefono resta com'è.

1 · Immersivo

On a hero page the art starts at the very top of the window: the header turns transparent (just a dark shade at the top so it reads), the tabs become glass pills on the art, the active one white in the hero's colour. The hero loses its box and fades into the page. Pages without a hero stay exactly as today. It's the phone's approach brought to desktop.

2 · Barra unica

The two top rows become one: logo, tabs and search/account in a single bar (the search narrower). On a hero page the bar takes a deep shade of the hero's colour and the hero hangs right under it as a full-width band with rounded bottom corners — bar and hero read as one piece. Without a hero: the same bar in red, then the page.

3 · Cornice tinta

Today's structure, untouched: red header, white tab row, the hero as a rounded box. On a hero page the frame around it takes the hero's colour — the header's gradient, the active tab, a line under the tab row, a soft glow of the colour behind the hero, its shadow tinted. The hero stops looking pasted onto a red page. Without a hero: exactly as today.
